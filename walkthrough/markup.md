# Crop, resize and mark up

- Crop: pick a ratio, drag a box or type X, Y, W and H, then press Enter.
- Resize: type a width or a height in pixels, or a percent.
- Arrow, Box, Text, Step: pick a colour and drag on the image. Each click of the Step tool places the next number.
- Blur and Pixelate: drag a box over anything private. Saving replaces those pixels for good.

Cmd+Z and Cmd+Shift+Z (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y on Windows and Linux) undo and redo each change.
